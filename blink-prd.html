Product requirements · portable desktop pet

Blink Desk Buddy

A tiny, round 3D buddy with big sparkly eyes that lives at the edge of your screen. It does a funny bit now and then, answers simple commands, changes outfits on its own from time to time, and goes quiet the moment you start working. It runs from a folder, with no installer and no admin rights.

1

Problem and goals

People spend 8 or more hours a day in front of the same desktop. Long stretches of focused work are tiring, and most "fun" software asks for attention through notifications, sounds or pop-ups. Classic desktop pets are charming but usually need an installer, block clicks, or wander across the document you are reading.

Blink is a light, portable companion that adds small moments of delight and gentle break reminders, and is built from day one to never interrupt work. Being able to forget it is there matters as much as how cute it is.

Goals

  • G1Make users smile at least once a day with antics, emotions and outfit changes.
  • G2Zero work disruption: no focus stealing, no blocked clicks, hidden during presentations and calls.
  • G3Respond to simple typed commands in under 200 ms.
  • G4Run on any Windows, macOS or Linux desktop from a single unzipped folder, without admin rights.

Non-goals (v1)

  • N1A productivity assistant or chatbot. Blink knows about 25 commands, not open conversation.
  • N2Reading screen content, documents or keystrokes.
  • N3Cloud accounts, sync or a paid skin store.
  • N4Mobile or tablet versions.

Character decision

One round, chibi-proportioned body (big head, small body, stubby arms) with a screen face, dressed in skins. A screen face draws any emotion cleanly at small sizes. Skins swap colours and add accessories (cat ears, bunny ears, bows, hats, tails), so users get a robot, kitty, bunny or panda from the same rig and animations.

3

Design principles

Visible, never in the way

Large enough to read its face at a glance (96 px default), small enough to cover under 0.5% of a 1080p screen. It lives on edges: the taskbar, window title bars, screen corners. Never the middle of the focused window.

Never takes focus

The overlay window cannot be activated. Typing always goes where it went before. Clicks pass through everywhere except Blink's own pixels.

Antics are earned

Default rate is one antic every 6 to 12 minutes, only while the user is idle for at least 3 seconds, never during typing, dragging, fullscreen or calls. Outfit changes follow the same rules.

Cute, colourful and light

Soft pastel palettes, round shapes, blush and sparkle in the eyes. Skins are tiny text files with colours and accessory names, so 9 skins add under 30 KB.

4

Character, emotions and skins

Blink's face is a curved screen drawn in real time: big eyes with two sparkle highlights, rosy cheeks and a small mouth. Emotions are shapes and motion, not texture swaps, and every skin tints the eyes and screen. Every emotion has a trigger, a duration and a way back to Content. Click any card to show it on the model at the top.

Skins and wardrobe

Nine skins ship at launch. Hover a buddy to say hi, click one to dress the model at the top of the page. Two of them put themselves on automatically: Snow Buddy in the holidays and Sleepy Cap at night.

Hover to say hi · click to wear

When outfits change

  • On request: wear the bunny, change outfit, or right-click › Wardrobe.
  • Daily (default on): a new outfit the first time you unlock each day.
  • Surprise (opt-in): an outfit change counts as an antic, at most once every 2 hours.
  • Seasonal (default on): holiday skins inside date windows, local calendar only.
  • Night (default on): Sleepy Cap from 9 pm to 6 am.

Outfit change animation

Blink spins, shrinks into a puff of colour-matched confetti, swaps skin at the midpoint and pops back with a little overshoot and a happy face. It lasts 1.2 s and never happens during Hush or Hidden.

Skin file format

skins/kitty.toml, about 1–2 KB: body, belly, trim, accent and eye colours, mouth style, and a list of accessories (catEars, tail). Accessories are built from simple 3D shapes in code, so no meshes ship for v1 skins. Custom .glb accessories are P2.

Anatomy

Face screen
Wide curved display, 2:1. Eyes, sparkle, brows (lid cuts), blush, mouth, tears, Z's and symbols are drawn procedurally.
Proportions
Chibi: head about 1.6× the body width, stubby arms, round boots.
Belly heart
Glowing heart on a lighter tummy patch. Beats slowly, dims in Hush mode.
Accessories
Antenna, ear discs, cat or bunny ears, bows, leaf sprout, hats, scarf and tails all come from the skin and wobble with motion.

Animation rules

Always on
Breathing bob, blinking every 2–6 s, eyes and head follow the cursor within 400 px, ears and tails sway.
Squash and stretch
On jumps and landings, volume-preserving (x/z widen as y shrinks).
Return to rest
Every action eases back to the idle pose. No snapping.
Reduced motion
If the OS asks for reduced motion, Blink only blinks and changes faces. No walking, spinning or confetti.

    Packaging without admin rights
    
    
    
      
    Blink ships as a zip (Windows), a signed .app in a zip (macOS) and an AppImage (Linux). Nothing is written outside the folder and the user profile. Users can run it from Downloads, the Desktop or a USB stick.

    What we never do
    
        
      
          
    • No installer, MSI, UAC prompt or service.
      • 
          
    • No global keyboard hooks. Busy detection uses idle time only, which also avoids antivirus false positives.
      • 
          
    • No writes to Program Files, HKLM or system folders.
      • 
          
    • Autostart is opt-in, using the per-user Run key (HKCU\…\Run), a user LaunchAgent, or ~/.config/autostart.
      • 
          
    • Uninstall is deleting the folder, plus the settings folder if wanted.
      • 
        
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
    Windows 10 / 11
      
        
    • Window styles WS_EX_LAYERED | WS_EX_TRANSPARENT | WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW | WS_EX_NOACTIVATE | WS_EX_TOPMOST.
      • 
        
    • Busy: GetLastInputInfo. Fullscreen or presenting: SHQueryUserNotificationState.
      • 
        
    • Code-signed to avoid SmartScreen blocks.
    
      
    macOS 12+
      
        
    • Borderless NSPanel at floating level, ignoresMouseEvents toggled by hit test.
      • 
        
    • Signed and notarized so Gatekeeper opens it without admin.
      • 
        
    • No Accessibility or Screen Recording permission needed for P0.
    
      
    Linux
      
        
    • Single AppImage, chmod +x and run.
      • 
        
    • X11: input shape region for click-through.
      • 
        
    • Wayland: layer-shell on KDE and wlroots. GNOME falls back to a small always-on-top window.

    12
    Privacy and trust
    
    
    
      
    Offline by default
    No network calls in v1. No accounts. Seasonal and Night skins use the local clock only. Opt-in crash reports, with the payload shown before sending.
    
      
    Coarse signals only
    Blink knows that you typed recently, never what. It reads window bounds, not window contents. It never captures the screen.
    
      
    IT friendly
    A blink.toml next to the exe can lock settings (e.g. force Calm, allow only certain skins, disable sounds). Published SHA-256 hashes and a signed binary make allow-listing simple.
